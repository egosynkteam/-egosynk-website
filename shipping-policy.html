Shipping Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Where we ship

We deliver across India to any serviceable pincode. We don't ship internationally yet.

Shipping charges

Order value (after discounts)Shipping
₹999 and aboveFree

The exact charge is shown in your bag before you pay and on your GST invoice.

Delivery time

  • Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 business days.
  • Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Remote areas, public holidays and courier delays can add time. We'll keep you posted.

Tracking

Once your order ships, the courier name and tracking number appear in My Account → Orders.

Payment

All orders are prepaid online via Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets). Cash on delivery isn't available at the moment.

Failed or returned deliveries

If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong or nobody was available after the courier's attempts, we'll contact you to re-ship it. Re-shipping may be charged at ₹79.
