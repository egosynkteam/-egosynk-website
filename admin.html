EGOSYNKStore admin. Sign in to manage orders, products and settings.

Enter your admin email. If it has an account, we'll email a reset link that works for 1 hour.

Owner recovery: the setup key is ADMIN_SECRET in Netlify → Environment variables. It resets the owner's password and signs that account out everywhere.

Choose a new password. Every device signed in to this account will be signed out.

First-time setup: create the owner account. Enter the current admin key once to prove it's you; after this the key stops working and everyone signs in with email + password.

Needs attention

Paid orders only. Revenue is what customers paid (incl. GST and shipping). Visitor and traffic numbers live in Google Analytics once it's connected in Settings.

Products

Categories

Order here = order on the store. Hiding a category hides its products too. GST default applies to every product in the category unless the product overrides it. The ID can't change after products use it.

Reviews

Only verified buyers (order ID + order email) can submit. Approved reviews show on the product page and in Google results. A public reply shows under the review.

Launch bundles

Offers shown on the HYDRO page. Coming sends people to the drop list. To sell one, create it as a product in Products (so the bag and checkout work), then set it to Live and paste the end of its product page address (for example hydro-starter-17). Until you tick Price is final, the site labels the price "Indicative".

Batch tests

Lab results for each manufactured batch. The QR code on the tub opens egosynk.com/batch/<code>, which shows what the lab measured against the label. Drafts stay private until you tick Published.

Drop lists

People who joined a "Join the drop" list on the storefront before the product is on sale. Everyone gets the launch email; only those with the marketing opt-in may get other offers. Each sign-up gets a confirmation email once Resend is set up.

Bulk enquiries

From the bulk orders page. Each one is also emailed to egosynkteam@gmail.com once Resend is set up. The note is internal only.

Coupons

Codes are case-insensitive. Discounts come off the items (not shipping) and reduce the GST taxable value on the invoice. "Used" counts paid orders only. Expiry is end of that day, IST. "First order only" is checked at checkout against the email and phone of earlier paid orders. Codes ticked "On product page" or "Top bar" are public, so don't tick private codes.

Size guides

Shown from "Size guide" on a product. Pick a guide per category (Categories tab) or per product. Rows: one per line, columns separated by |.

Shipping (Shiprocket)

Default packed parcel per category: weight in grams, size in cm. The ship form adds up a mixed order and you can change it there. Couriers charge on the higher of actual and volumetric weight (L×B×H ÷ 5000).
